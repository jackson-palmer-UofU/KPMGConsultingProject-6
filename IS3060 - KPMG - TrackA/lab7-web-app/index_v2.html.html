Tracking Investment Research Requests

A centralized process for organizing investment research requests, assigning work to research teams, monitoring priorities, and improving visibility across the investment organization.

About the Process

The investment research request process connects Portfolio Managers and Senior Investment Analysts with Junior Financial Analysts and Quantitative Researchers. The goal is to create better visibility into research demand and ensure that the most important investment questions receive appropriate attention.

Portfolio Managers & Senior Analysts

Submit research questions, identify investment needs, communicate deadlines, and provide context for requested analysis.

Research Executors

Junior Financial Analysts and Quantitative Researchers review requests, perform analysis, gather data, and communicate progress or completed findings.

Research Leadership

The Head of Research or CIO monitors team capacity, competing priorities, request volume, and overall research output.

Basic Research Request Flow

1

Submit

A PM or Senior Analyst identifies an investment research question and communicates the requested analysis.

2

Review

The research team reviews the request, determines its priority, and identifies an appropriate analyst or researcher.

3

Analyze

The assigned team member conducts the research, gathers relevant information, and develops the requested analysis.

4

Deliver

Findings are returned to the requester and the research request is marked as complete for visibility across the team.

How the New Queue Improves the Process

  • Creates a single location for active research requests.
  • Makes ownership and expected completion dates visible.
  • Allows users to identify high-priority requests quickly.
  • Search and sector filtering can help identify related or potentially duplicate research requests.
  • Leadership can use the queue to understand workload and research team capacity.

Current Process Information

The original process is primarily informal. Requests may arrive through email, instant messaging, or conversations, making it difficult to maintain a complete and shared view of research activity.

Original investment research process information
Process Area Current Situation Impact Status
Request Submission Requests are commonly submitted through email, Slack, Teams, or informal conversations. Requests can be difficult to locate and track. Current
Work Visibility There is no single queue showing which analyst owns each request. PMs and leadership may have difficulty estimating completion times. Needs Review
Duplicate Research Similar requests can be submitted by multiple investment professionals. Analysts may repeat work that was recently completed. Needs Review
Priority Management Urgent opportunities may compete with routine research requests. Important research may not receive attention at the appropriate time. Needs Review
Research Completion Completed work is generally delivered directly to the original requester. Completed research may be difficult for other team members to discover later. Current

Current Process Focus

The basic process is centered on receiving and fulfilling individual research requests. The primary opportunity for improvement is creating greater visibility into request ownership, priority, workload, and completion status while reducing unnecessary duplication.
